Overflight Watch — Radar Simulation with Time Travel

This is a simulation of an overflight tracker. It generates realistic aircraft movements around your chosen place and lets you explore closest-approach history for the whole time window.

Time travel bar

Use the time slider below the simulation clock to travel back through the collected history. Move the slider to any point in the last H hours and the map + list will show exactly which flights were active at that moment. The ⏪ 10 min button jumps you exactly 10 minutes back from the current position — click it repeatedly to step backwards in time.

Simulation clock

The simulation time shows the moment currently displayed. When the slider is at the far right, you are watching the live simulation. Drag it left to rewind.

Finding a place (e.g. Mandra Attikis)

Type a place name into the search box. In simulation mode, the built-in geocoder knows the coordinates of common places like Mandra Attikis (Mandra, Attica). You can also enter coordinates directly as lat,lon.

Map tiles

The map uses CARTO's dark basemap. If it appears blank, check your internet connection or try reloading — the flight list and simulation continue to work without the map.

Good to know

  • Click any aircraft on the map or in the list to see its details and altitude profile.
  • Change radius or hours to re-filter the simulated history.
  • Export/Import works with the simulated data (JSON archive of snapshots).
  • The URL updates as you move — copy it to share the exact view.
